Where the money goes

Two decades of spending growth, broken down by who it pays.

Where did the money go? — 20 years of payroll →
Labor is roughly nine of every ten dollars a district spends, so this is very nearly the whole spending story. FY2005–FY2025 payroll — salaries and benefits — split into teachers, paraeducators, school support, school administration and central administration, and set against enrollment. Labor rose 163% while enrollment rose 41%; central administration grew nearly five-fold, more than twice as fast as the district. After inflation, spending per student is up 13%.
